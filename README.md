# micah lai

**Engineering · Film · Design · Photography**

Hey! I'm a student at Cornell University studying Electrical & Computer
Engineering. I'm interested in CMOS sensor hardware and computational
engineering — which stems from my love for film & art.

I graduated from Orange County School of the Arts in the Film & Television
program. I mainly work as a cinematographer and 1st AC on narratives, plus
some industrial and freelance work on the side.

In off hours: guitar, piano, dancing, and rock climbing.

→ **[micahlai.com](https://micahlai.com)**

---

## What I build

Most of it sits where instruments, sensors, and software meet.

| | |
|---|---|
| **[MIDI Controller Pedal](https://github.com/micahlai/midi-guitar-pedal)** | A scratch-built 10-footswitch MIDI pedalboard for live guitar rigs, on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W. `hardware` `embedded` `python` |
| **[Guitar Companion](https://github.com/micahlai/guitar-companion)** | Native macOS practice studio — song analysis, stem isolation, beat-snapped looping, recorded takes. Swift/SwiftUI + CoreAudio + Accelerate, 200+ unit tests. `swift` `dsp` `ml` |
| **[Screen Mosaic](https://github.com/micahlai/screen-mosaic)** | One phone photo calibrates a pile of laptops, tablets, and phones into a single continuous canvas. Built at the UC Berkeley 2026 AI Hackathon. `computer-vision` `opencv` — [demo](https://youtu.be/9e3s38W8MYw) · [writeup](https://devpost.com/software/screen-mosaic) |
| **[Clipboard — OR Monitor](https://github.com/micahlai/pih)** | Revision-tracked OR schedule editor + wall display replacing a paper clipboard at PIH Health Whittier. `dotnet` `maui` `aspnet-core` |
| **[mini-midi](https://github.com/micahlai/mini-midi)** | Three-footswitch USB MIDI controller on an RP2040, configured from the browser over Web Serial. `c++` `rp2040` — [try it](https://micahlai.github.io/mini-midi/) |
| **[Home Lab](https://github.com/micahlai/home-lab)** | Wake-on-demand home server: an always-on Pi wakes the hibernating family PC over a mesh VPN. `raspberry-pi` `networking` |
| **[Dance App](https://github.com/micahlai/dance-practice-app)** | iPad DJ deck for dance practice — on-device tempo detection (vDSP), beat-grid scrubbing and looping. `swift` `dsp` |
| **[Musical Fingers](https://github.com/JaidenAGrimminck/makeathon)** | Instrument gloves that turn finger taps into a live multi-track looper. Cornell 2026 Hardware Hackathon. `embedded` `websockets` |
